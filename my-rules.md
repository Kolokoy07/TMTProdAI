- Titles (section or level labels) sit in the middle of the frame. They build in slowly and stay
  fully on screen at least 3 seconds, longer for titles with more words (this replaced an earlier
  5-second cap).
  "can you make the titles appear only for max 5 seconds in the middle of the frame" (2026-10-25)
  "the animation of the title is too fast, make it stay atleast 3 seconds depending on length of words" (2026-10-25)
- Text hooks build in per word like the titles and stay on screen 4 to just under 5 seconds (never 5 s or
  longer, never the whole video), unless that would cover action in the footage; then the hook ends when the
  action starts. Coaching Calls hooks: 4.5 s.
  "make that as well for text hooks ok? make it stay atleast 4-5 seconds if it is not on top of any action in the video" (2026-10-25)
  "hooks should be less than 5 seconds in duration ok? remember that rule from before?" (2026-10-26)
- Captions are short and one line only.
  "also make the captions shorter and 1 line only" (2026-10-25)
- Titles animate in per word with smooth, premium ease-ins.
  "I want the titles to animate in per word, smooth ease ins like a premium motion style" (2026-10-25)
- Tight cuts: remove every stutter and retake, keep only a short breath between phrases.
