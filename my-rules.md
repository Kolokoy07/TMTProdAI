Lasting rules from the user's notes. They beat every style in `styles/`, and styles beat `my-style.md`.
Each rule is followed by the user's own words and the date. Rules are grouped by the kind of video they
apply to; "Every edit" applies to all of them.

## Which style for which video

| Video | Style | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Talking-head reels | Signature Gold (`styles/DEFAULT`) | Default for "edit this" |
| Screen recordings with a camera bubble, cut into reels | Screen-share split (`styles/screen-share-split.md`) | Plain versions: Simple split, Simple split (no hook) |
| Ads for a product (for example the MATE Portal ads) | Screen-share split look, split screen | Follow the user's script |
| Coaching calls to students and traders | Coaching Calls (`styles/coaching-calls.md`) | Split screen for charts and student talking |
| Horizontal lessons and episodes | Long-form rules below | No style file |

- Keep the formats separate. Split-screen layout, screen zoom-ins, and small cards beside the speaker
  belong only to the split styles; never mix them into Signature Gold.

## Vertical videos (reels, shorts, ads)

- Safe zone: nothing important in the bottom ~390 px or the right-edge button column. That covers
  graphics, captions, and cards.
  "I am worried about the graphics at the bottom of the video, it will be blocked by social media, its not on the safe zone" (2026-10-25)
- Captions are short and one line only.
  "also make the captions shorter and 1 line only" (2026-10-25)
- Text hooks build in per word like titles. They stay 4 s to just under 5 s: never 5 s or longer, never
  the whole video. If that would cover action in the footage, the hook ends when the action starts.

  "hooks should be less than 5 seconds in duration ok? remember that rule from before?" (2026-10-26)
