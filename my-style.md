# My style

- Format: vertical 9:16 (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)
- Captions: full-sentence subtitles
- Caption position: lower third
- Caption colors: white with shadow
- Hook: big hook text in the first 3 seconds
- Title fonts: cinematic pairing like the user's "Cinematic fonts" reference (2026-10-25): a heavy,
  tightly spaced sans word with a soft white-to-silver gradient (Inter Black), an overlapping
  lowercase italic serif (Instrument Serif Italic) in gold (#f5c84c, option C picked 2026-10-25),
  and a small bold sans line. No boxes; a soft
  dark band behind the text when the background is light. Used for hook, section titles, and CTA.
- Pace: tight, cut every breath and pause
- Graphics: minimal
- Color: natural
- Sound: sound effects plus a quiet music bed (music and effects from my-sounds/)
  - Approved 2026-10-25: a riser under the text hook that lands on an impact when the first title
    enters; soft per-word swishes on title words, a lighter shimmer on the serif words, a low swell
    when a title's band appears, a gentle reverse swish when it leaves; pops on the spoken CTA keyword.
  - Music bed about 20 dB under the voice, ducked further under speech. If my-sounds/ has no
    track, a generated ambient pad is fine.
