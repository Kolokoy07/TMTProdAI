# Style: Simple split (no hook)

Simple split without the opening hook (2026-10-26). Machine-readable values: `styles/simple-split-nohook.json`
(`hook.layout` "none"). Same scripts as Screen-share split (`styles/screen-share-split/`).

- Everything as in `simple-split.md` (split screen edge to edge on a faint grid with soft glows, screen
  zoom-ins, one-line captions), except: no hook text, no hook sounds, captions from the first word, and
  the screen zooms in from 0.6 s. Voice only.
