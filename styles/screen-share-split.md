# Style: Screen-share split

Built with the user on the Coach Tim Mate Portal reels (2026-10-25/26). Machine-readable values:
`styles/screen-share-split.json`. Scripts and sounds: `styles/screen-share-split/` (see its README).

Use for: screen recordings that have the speaker in a camera bubble (tutorials, platform walkthroughs),
cut into educational reels. Talking-head reels keep using the default style (Organic Ad style).

- Format: 9:16 1080x1920, split screen. Screen share on top (1000x720 glass window), the speaker
  always in the bottom panel (1000x490), captions on the seam between them.
- Speaker panel: the camera bubble cropped from the 4K recording, following it to whichever corner it
  is in; the bubble inside the screen share is covered so the speaker never shows twice. Full-screen
  camera shots show the speaker centred below and a glass title card on top.
- Screen share: zoomed about 1.3x on where things happen (cursor, typing, new text), moving only when
  the action moves; each new section opens wide, then zooms in; a whole line of text stays in view.
- Safe zone: nothing important below y 1530 or right of x 960 (Reels/TikTok buttons and caption).
- Colours (gold): headline gradient #ffffff to #b9b9b9, accent #f5c84c, captions #ffffff, shade
  #06070a, green glass glow #2bd46a
- Fonts (signature): headline Inter 900; accent Instrument Serif italic; captions Inter 800
- Hook: 4.6 s, word-blur-rise, big heavy word + gold serif line + small question line; riser into impact
- Captions: one line, max 26 characters, 56px, words rise out of a blur as spoken
- Topic chip: small glass "MATE PORTAL / <topic>" label in the speaker panel's top-left corner
- Cards: small glass cards (eyebrow + 2-4 ticked rows, or a big word + serif line) left of the speaker,
  anchored to the words they explain; never over the speaker
- Look: liquid glass panels over drifting green/gold glows, natural grade, tight cuts, no speed-ups
- Sound: premium SFX (whoosh on section jumps, swell + pops on cards, swishes on hook words); ambient
  pad about 20 dB under the voice, ducked under speech
- Reels: at least 40 s each, one topic per reel, lines may be reordered so it flows; show the topic
  list to the user before cutting
