# Style: Simple split

Plain version of Screen-share split, made for the Coach Tim reels (2026-10-26). Machine-readable values:
`styles/simple-split.json`. Same scripts as Screen-share split (`styles/screen-share-split/`), with
`layout.simple` on.

Use for: screen recordings with the speaker in a camera bubble, when the user wants plain clips.

- Format: 9:16 1080x1920 split screen on black. Screen share edge to edge on top (1080x780 from y 150),
  the speaker edge to edge below (1080x560 from y 1040), captions in the black band between them.
- Screen share: same auto zoom-ins as Screen-share split (about 1.3x on where things happen).
- Hook: the same as Screen-share split (big word, gold italic line, small question line, 4.6 s, riser into
  an impact and per-word swishes). Captions start after it: one line, max 26 characters, words rise in.
- A faint grid and slow-drifting blurred green and gold glows on the black. No liquid glass, topic chip,
  cards, or title card. Full-screen camera shots show the
  camera on top and the speaker crop below.
- Sound: the hook sounds only, then voice; no music bed. See `simple-split-nohook.md` for the version
  without a hook.
